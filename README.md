## 🎮 Gameplay

Your village is small but full of potential. Enemies attack in waves. Manage resources, train units, and defend your home. Every decision matters — one wrong move and the village falls.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Engine:** Unity
- **Language:** C#
- **Platforms:** Windows, Android
